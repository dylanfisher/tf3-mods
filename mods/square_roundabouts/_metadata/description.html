Build a complete roundabout in one click: the ring, its approach roads and, if you like, splitter islands, a road or tram line through the middle (a hamburger roundabout, optionally with experimental traffic lights) and a landscaped centre island. Round or oval, mini or huge, three to six arms. Three more menu entries build a Magic Roundabout (five mini roundabouts around a two-way ring), a Channelized Junction (a T or X junction with slip lanes across the corners) and a Roundabout Interchange (a highway junction with a roundabout on another level). Three more, next to the game's own interchanges, build highway interchanges with one-way ramps: Parclo, Parclo, two quadrants (both partial cloverleafs) and Diamond.

Once placed, everything turns into ordinary roads that you can edit, extend or connect like any other street.

Features

  • Five sizes: XS 10 m, S 15 m, M 20 m, L 35 m or XL 60 m radius (extra small to extra large). XS is a mini roundabout with a flat island. If the roads you chose don't fit (wide arms on a small ring, splitter islands, a road through the centre), the ring grows to the smallest size where they do. The sizes were checked against the game's own placement check, combination by combination.
  • Round or oval: ovals from 1.25 to 2 times longer than wide.
  • Arms: 3 (T or Y layout), 4, 5, 6 or none, with town or country roads in small, medium or large. Each arm is 30 m long, ready to connect.
  • Ring lanes: 1, 2 or 4, using the game's own one-way roads, the same ones as the built-in roundabout.
  • Both directions: counterclockwise for right-hand traffic, clockwise for left-hand traffic.
  • Splitter islands: each arm splits into a one-way entry and exit before the ring, with a small island between them when a centre island is chosen. Medium and large arm roads split into wider one-way roads and need a bigger ring: 20 m or more for medium arms (40 m on a 1-lane ring or with 5 or 6 arms), 40 m or more for large arms (60 m with 5 or 6 arms on 1 or 2 lanes).
  • Through the centre: a straight road or tram line across the middle, splitting the centre island in two. A tram line has tracks only along that straight line: its two arms and across the centre. On a small ring the tram road runs all the way across.
  • Hamburger roundabout: the road through the centre can be a different road from the arms, so a large road cuts straight through a smaller roundabout, optionally with traffic lights where it crosses the ring (experimental).
  • Centre island: none, grass, grass with trees, a fountain or paving. The island keeps town buildings out.
  • Magic Roundabout: five mini roundabouts on a two-way central ring, after the famous one in Swindon. Traffic can go round either way. Five sizes, from XS to XL, with 1 or 2 lanes on the mini roundabouts.
  • Channelized Junction: a T or X junction of a main and a side road with one-way slip lanes that bypass it across the corners, triangle islands between them and optional splitter islands on the side road. Built for right- or left-hand traffic.
  • Roundabout Interchange: a highway crossed by a roundabout junction on another level, with four slip roads. Three layouts (a two-bridge ring, a dumbbell, a peanut), the ring over the highway or the highway over the ring, three highway sizes. The bridges are steel before 1970 and concrete after.
  • Parclo: a partial cloverleaf between a highway and a road. Ramps in all four quadrants, loops in two of them, the other ramps straight and wrapped around the loops. Layout A has loop on-ramps, Layout B loop exits.
  • Parclo, two quadrants: ramps in two quadrants only, a loop and a straight ramp in each. Layout A, B or AB (both quadrants on one side of the highway).
  • Diamond: four straight ramps, one per quadrant, no loops.
  • All three highway interchanges: the road over the highway or the highway over the road, three highway sizes, three scales of the whole footprint, right- or left-hand traffic, steel bridges before 1970 and concrete after.
  • Tram tracks on the ring and every arm.
  • Experimental: traffic lights at the ring entries.
  • Uses only base-game roads, trees and models.

The settings sit in the panel on the left of the screen while you place an entry. Short button labels are explained below and in each setting's tooltip: S, M, L mean small, medium, large; XS and XL extra small and extra large.

Settings: Better Roundabouts

  • Shape (default Round): Round, 1.25:1, 1.5:1, 1.75:1 or 2:1 (an oval that many times longer than wide).
  • Ring lanes (default 2): 1, 2 or 4.
  • Radius (default XS): XS 10 m, S 15 m, M 20 m, L 35 m or XL 60 m, the size of the roundabout, measured inside the ring road (across the narrow side of an oval). It is a minimum: the ring grows if the chosen roads don't fit.
  • Arms (default 4, a drop-down): 3 (T), 3 (Y), 4, 5, 6 or None.
  • Splitter islands (default Off).
  • Arm road (default Town, small): town or country, small, medium or large. Arms are 30 m long, from the outer edge of the ring or, with splitter islands, from the split.
  • Through the centre (default Nothing, a drop-down): Road, Tram line or Road + traffic lights, between two opposite arms. Layouts without opposite arms get one extra arm opposite the first; Arms None gets two. Tram line puts tram tracks on its two arms and across the centre, not on the ring. Road + traffic lights (experimental) is the same road with traffic lights where it crosses the ring, from 1950; it may have no effect yet.
  • Through road (default Same as arms): the road type of the road through the centre, town or country, small, medium or large. A road wider than the arms makes the ring grow to fit. No effect with Nothing or Tram line.
  • Tram tracks (default Off): electrified tracks on the ring, every arm, the splitter lanes and a through road.
  • Direction (default CCW): CCW (counterclockwise) for right-hand traffic, CW (clockwise) for left-hand traffic.
  • Centre island (default None): None, Grass, Grass and trees, Fountain or Paved. Also used for the splitter islands. Mini roundabouts get paving instead of trees or a fountain.
  • Traffic lights (default Off; experimental): On asks for traffic lights where traffic enters the ring, from 1950. It may have no effect yet.

Settings: Magic Roundabout

  • Size (default XS): XS, S, M, L or XL (extra small to extra large), with mini roundabouts of 8, 10, 12, 20 or 35 m radius and the central ring between them. Wide arm roads need a larger size and grow to fit (Town, large and Country, large need at least L).
  • Arm road (default Town, small): the road of the five 30 m approach arms.
  • Mini roundabout lanes (default 1): 1 or 2 lanes on each mini roundabout. 2 lanes need size L or bigger, so smaller sizes grow to L. The central ring keeps one lane each way.
  • Direction (default CCW): the direction of the mini roundabouts, CCW (counterclockwise) for right-hand traffic or CW (clockwise) for left-hand traffic. The central ring is two-way, so its inner lane runs the other way.
  • Centre island (default None): the island inside the central ring and the mini roundabouts. Minis below 15 m get paving instead of trees or a fountain.

Settings: Channelized Junction

  • Layout (default T): T (one side road) or X (side roads on both sides).
  • Main road (default Town, medium) and Side road (default Town, small): town or country, small, medium or large.
  • Slip lanes (default Near-side corners, a drop-down): Near-side corners (the short turns: right turns in right-hand traffic, left turns in left-hand traffic), All turns off the main road, or None. Wider roads get slip lanes at a steeper angle.
  • Side-road splitter islands (default Off): splits each side road into a one-way entry and exit where it meets the main road. With slip lanes the junction gets much larger.
  • Arm length (default 30 m): 30, 40, 50 or 60 m, how far each road runs on beyond the outermost slip lane or split.
  • Traffic side (default Right): Right (right-hand traffic) or Left (left-hand traffic); left-hand is the mirror image.
  • Islands (default None): None, Grass or Paved, for the triangle islands and the splitter islands.

Settings: Roundabout Interchange

  • Layout (default Two-bridge ring, a drop-down): Two-bridge ring (one ring around the highway, crossing it on two bridges), Dumbbell (a ring each side, joined by a two-way bridge) or Peanut (one one-way loop with a lobe each side and a narrow waist of two one-way bridges).
  • Highway (default M): S, M or L (small, medium or large), built as two one-way carriageways.
  • On top (default Ring): Ring (the roundabout 8 m up, on bridges where it crosses the highway) or Hwy (the highway 8 m up, on a bridge across the roundabout).
  • Ring lanes (default 2): 1, 2 or 4.
  • Radius (default S): S 15 m, M 20 m, L 30 m or XL 45 m, inside each ring road. A minimum: the rings grow if the roads don't fit, and a two-bridge ring starts at 25 to 40 m to span the highway (35 to 55 m with 4 ring lanes).
  • Cross road (default Town, medium): the two-way road across the highway (its two arms, and the dumbbell's bridge).
  • Slip road length (default 140 m): 120, 140, 160 or 180 m, along the highway from the ring to where a slip road joins it. Shorter is steeper.
  • Arm length (default 30 m): 30, 40, 50 or 60 m, how far the cross road runs on beyond the ring, and the highway beyond the slip roads.
  • Traffic side (default Right): Right (right-hand traffic, rings counterclockwise) or Left (left-hand traffic): everything mirrored, with the rings clockwise.
  • Centre island (default None): None, Grass, Grass and trees, Fountain or Paved, inside each ring at ground level.

Settings: Parclo, Parclo two quadrants and Diamond

  • Layout (Parclo: A or B, default A; Parclo, two quadrants: A, B or AB, default A; Diamond has none): A puts the loops in the quadrants before the cross road (loop on-ramps), B in the quadrants after it (loop exits). AB, two quadrants only: both quadrants on one side of the highway.
  • Highway (default M): S, M or L (small, medium or large), built as two one-way carriageways with 1, 2 or 3 lanes each way.
  • On top (default Road): Road (the cross road 8 m up on a bridge, the highway at ground level) or Hwy (the highway 8 m up on two bridges, the cross road at ground level). The ramps climb the 8 m.
  • Cross road (default Town, medium): the two-way road across the highway, town or country, small, medium or large.
  • Scale (default S): S, M or L (small, medium or large), the size of the whole footprint (M about 1.6 times, L 2.5 times S). Parclo loops have a radius of 25, 45 or 80 m. Diamond ramps meet the cross road 0, 40 or 100 m beyond the bridge and run 120, 192 or 300 m along the highway. A minimum: it grows if the roads don't fit.
  • Ramp length (default Short): Short, Medium or Long, adding 0, 30 or 60 m to the ramps along the highway. Longer ramps are gentler. At Scale L the parclo ramps stop at the longest the game accepts, so Medium and Long may build the same.
  • Arm length (default 30 m): 30, 45 or 60 m, how far the cross road runs on beyond the ramps, and the highway beyond the outermost ramps.
  • Traffic side (default Right): Right (right-hand traffic) or Left (left-hand traffic); left-hand mirrors everything.

Companion mods: Smart Lanes and Bulk Bulldozer

Roundabouts & Interchanges pairs well with two separate mods of mine. To get them, view my other mods (click the creator name, square) and subscribe. Each mod works independently, so you can use any of them on its own.

Smart Lanes: with it, every multi-lane roundabout, splitter island and slip lane you build here gets proper lane discipline straight away: entry and exit lanes matched one to one, and the outer ring lane exiting at the next exit. Without it, the game's own lane settings apply.

Bulk Bulldozer: a bulldoze brush for easy clean-up. Once built, a roundabout is many separate road pieces. To replace an old junction, or to rebuild a roundabout with other settings, paint over it with Bulk Bulldozer, check the red highlight and cost, and remove it all in one go instead of piece by piece. Find it in Road → Tools.

Notes

  • Find "Better Roundabouts", "Magic Roundabout", "Channelized Junction" and "Roundabout Interchange" in the road construction menu next to the built-in roundabout. "Parclo" and "Parclo, two quadrants" are next to the game's cloverleaf, "Diamond" next to the game's diamond interchange. All seven are available from 1940 (the game's own interchanges from 1960). The mod's menu icons carry a small badge so you can tell them from the game's.
  • Rotate it while placing with the game's usual construction rotation keys. The arm ends are loose road ends: connect your roads to them.
  • The roundabout cannot be reconfigured after it is built, because it has already become plain roads. Bulldoze and place it again to change it.
  • The Channelized Junction, the Roundabout Interchange and the three highway interchanges have been checked against the game's placement check on flat ground, less thoroughly than the roundabouts: if a combination says "Construction not possible", try other spacing (another radius, scale or ramp length) or another road type. The highway interchanges are large, especially at Scale L: leave room around them.
  • Adds nothing to the game but seven construction menu entries, and runs no scripts while you play. Safe to add to an existing save. If you remove the mod, the roads stay; centre islands that were placed may be missing.
