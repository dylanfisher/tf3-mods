Build a complete roundabout in one click: the ring, its approach roads and, if you like, splitter islands, a road or tram line through the middle (a hamburger roundabout, optionally with experimental traffic lights) and a landscaped centre island. Round or oval, mini or huge, three to six arms. Three more menu entries build a Magic Roundabout (five mini roundabouts around a two-way ring), a Channelized Junction (a T or X junction with slip lanes across the corners) and a Roundabout Interchange (a highway junction with a roundabout on another level). Three more, next to the game's own interchanges, build highway interchanges with one-way ramps: Parclo, Parclo, two quadrants (both partial cloverleafs) and Diamond.

Once placed, everything turns into ordinary roads that you can edit, extend or connect like any other street.

Features

  • Better Roundabouts: round or oval (up to 2:1), five sizes from a mini roundabout (XS, 10 m) to XL (60 m), 1, 2 or 4 ring lanes, 3 (T or Y), 4, 5, 6 or no arms, clockwise or counterclockwise. Optional splitter islands, a road or tram line through the centre (a hamburger roundabout, the through road can differ from the arms) and a centre island: grass, grass with trees, a fountain or paving. If the roads you chose don't fit, the ring grows to the smallest size where they do.
  • Magic Roundabout: five mini roundabouts on a two-way central ring, after the famous one in Swindon. Five sizes, 1 or 2 lanes on the minis.
  • Channelized Junction: a T or X junction with one-way slip lanes across the corners, triangle islands and optional splitter islands on the side road. Five scales.
  • Roundabout Interchange: a highway crossed by a roundabout on another level, with four slip roads. Two-bridge ring, dumbbell or peanut; the ring over the highway or the highway over the ring.
  • Parclo, Parclo, two quadrants and Diamond: highway interchanges with one-way ramps, the road over the highway or the highway over the road, three scales and three ramp lengths.
  • Right- or left-hand traffic on every entry. Bridges are steel before 1970 and concrete after.
  • Experimental: traffic lights at the ring entries, or where a through road crosses the ring.
  • Uses only base-game roads, trees and models.

Supported roads

  • Town and country roads, small, medium and large, for arms, through roads, main and side roads and cross roads.
  • Highways, small, medium and large, built as two one-way carriageways (Roundabout Interchange, Parclo, Diamond).
  • One-way roads for the rings, splitter lanes, slip lanes and ramps: the game's own, the same ones as the built-in roundabout.
  • Tram tracks: on the ring and every arm, or a tram line straight through the centre.

The settings sit in the panel on the left of the screen while you place an entry; each setting's tooltip explains it. S, M, L mean small, medium, large; XS and XL extra small and extra large.

Companion mods: Smart Lanes and Bulk Bulldozer

Roundabouts & Interchanges pairs well with two separate mods of mine. To get them, view my other mods (click the creator name, square) and subscribe. Each mod works independently, so you can use any of them on its own.

Smart Lanes: with it, every multi-lane roundabout, splitter island and slip lane you build here gets proper lane discipline straight away: entry and exit lanes matched one to one, and the outer ring lane exiting at the next exit. Without it, the game's own lane settings apply.

Bulk Bulldozer: a bulldoze brush for easy clean-up. Once built, a roundabout is many separate road pieces. To replace an old junction, or to rebuild a roundabout with other settings, paint over it with Bulk Bulldozer, check the red highlight and cost, and remove it all in one go instead of piece by piece. Find it in Road → Tools.

Notes

  • Find "Better Roundabouts", "Magic Roundabout", "Channelized Junction" and "Roundabout Interchange" in the road construction menu next to the built-in roundabout. "Parclo" and "Parclo, two quadrants" are next to the game's cloverleaf, "Diamond" next to the game's diamond interchange. All seven are available from 1940 (the game's own interchanges from 1960). The mod's menu icons carry a small badge so you can tell them from the game's.
  • Rotate it while placing with the game's usual construction rotation keys. The arm ends are loose road ends: connect your roads to them.
  • The roundabout cannot be reconfigured after it is built, because it has already become plain roads. Bulldoze and place it again to change it.
  • The Channelized Junction, the Roundabout Interchange and the three highway interchanges have been checked against the game's placement check on flat ground, less thoroughly than the roundabouts: if a combination says "Construction not possible", try other spacing (another scale or ramp length) or another road type. The highway interchanges are large, especially at Scale L: leave room around them.
  • Adds nothing to the game but seven construction menu entries, and runs no scripts while you play. Safe to add to an existing save. If you remove the mod, the roads stay; centre islands that were placed may be missing.
