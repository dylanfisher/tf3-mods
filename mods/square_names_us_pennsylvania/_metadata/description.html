Play in Pennsylvania: 488 real towns from Philadelphia to Pittsburgh, real streets for your bus stops, and real names for train stations, airports and ferry landings.

Pick the name set "Pennsylvania (Towns & Streets)" for a new game. Then just build: every new Road Stop and station in a known city gets a real local name a moment after you place it.

Features

  • Pennsylvania towns: a name set with 488 real towns and places, from big cities to small boroughs, coal towns, river towns and Pocono villages (US Census Bureau data).
  • Towns that suit your map: a new game picks its towns by the map's climate. Temperate maps: mostly central Pennsylvania, plus the southeast (Philadelphia, Lehigh Valley), the southwest (Pittsburgh), the northeast (Scranton, Poconos) and the Lake Erie shore. Subarctic maps: mostly the Northern Tier (Allegheny forest), plus the northeast (Scranton, Poconos). Tropical maps: mostly the southeast (Philadelphia, Lehigh Valley), plus the southwest (Pittsburgh). Dry maps and climates from other mods use the whole state.
  • Real street names: new Road Stops in 13 cities (Philadelphia, Pittsburgh, Allentown, …) are named after their real streets, like Broad Street or Liberty Avenue: 372 streets in all, never twice in the same town.
  • Pennsylvania-style streets everywhere else: other towns draw their stop names from 137 street names in the local style (tree streets, borough squares, pikes, canals, coal patches and ridges).
  • Real station names: new train stations, airports and ferry landings in 54 cities get real names, like 30th Street, Steel Plaza or Philadelphia International Airport (58 rail stations, 49 airports, 2 ferry landings).
  • Neighbourhood names: new bus and tram stations in 2 cities are named after real neighbourhoods (109 in all).
  • Everything else follows: industries, depots and their stations are named after your Pennsylvania towns by the game itself.

Settings

  • Real street names (default On): renames new Road Stops in known cities after their real streets.
  • Real station names (default On): renames new train stations, bus and tram stations, airports and harbours in known cities.

Notes

  • Unlike a plain name set, this mod also renames the stops and stations you build after real streets, stations and neighbourhoods in their city.
  • Street and station names also work with any other name set, in towns that carry a Pennsylvania city's name (rename a town to Philadelphia and its next stop is on a real Philadelphia street).
  • Only renames what you build, right after you build it. Existing stops and stations are never touched, and neither are your own renames.
  • Cosmetic only, safe to add to an existing save. Never remove it from a game started with the "Pennsylvania (Towns & Streets)" name set: the save still loads, but the game crashes as soon as it needs a new name (building a road, for example). Turning the mod back on fixes the save. In any other save, removing it keeps the names already given and stops renaming.
  • Not affiliated with the "Pennsylvania Names" mod; all lists here are our own.

Credits
