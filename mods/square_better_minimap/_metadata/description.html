An overview map of your whole savegame in a small window. See where you are, spot every resource at a glance and jump anywhere with one click.

Open it with the Map button in the top-left button ridge.

Features

  • Your map: the terrain, water and towns of your save, with every industry shown by the icon of what it produces.
  • Roads and towns: tick Net to draw your streets, railway tracks and town buildings onto the map. It keeps itself up to date as you build or bulldoze, redraws only what changed, and costs nothing while switched off. Tick Hi-vis for bright, opaque colours (yellow streets, magenta tracks, orange towns) that stand out on any terrain.
  • Camera box: shows where the camera is looking and follows it as you pan and zoom.
  • View direction arrow: an arrow in the camera box points the way the camera faces.
  • Click to jump: click anywhere on the map to move the camera there.
  • Two sizes, small and large: the magnifier button switches between them. Pin the window to keep it open while you play.
  • Remembers its position, size, pin, industry, network and hi-vis toggles, also after loading a save. A pinned minimap opens again by itself.
  • Toggle the industry icons. The map updates on its own when the terrain, towns or industries change.

Settings

  • View direction arrow (default On): the arrow in the camera box.
  • Debug: performance log (default Off): writes timing lines to the game's log file every 30 s. Only for tracking down slowdowns.

Settings can be changed whenever you start or load a game.

Notes

  • Reads the game state only and never changes your save. Safe to add to or remove from an existing game.
  • The remembered window settings are stored in your game settings, not in the save, so they apply to every game.

Changelog

Revision 5 (2026-10-10)

  • New: the Net toggle draws streets, railway tracks and town buildings onto the map and keeps them up to date. Off by default.
  • New: the network is built in small steps spread over many frames and drawn as separate strips, so only the parts of the map that changed are redrawn. Building or bulldozing doesn't make the game stutter, and switching Net off and on again shows the map instantly.
  • New: the Hi-vis toggle draws the network in bright, opaque colours. Switching it is instant and doesn't read the map again.
  • Changed: the S/L size button is now a magnifier icon in the window header, and the header no longer shows a "Map" title.
  • Changed: the view direction arrow is now a slim white triangle.
  • New: an optional performance log for troubleshooting (mod setting "Debug: performance log", Off by default).

Revision 4 (2026-10-07)

  • Changed: no longer warns when removed from a save; it is safe to remove at any time.

Revision 3 (2026-10-07)

  • New: the minimap remembers its position, size, pin and industry toggle after you load a save or restart the game.
  • New: a minimap that was pinned and open opens again by itself after a load.
  • Changed: the small size (S) is smaller.
  • Changed: the size button now switches between small and large only; medium is gone.

Revision 2 (2026-10-07)

  • New: an arrow in the camera box that points where the camera is looking.
  • New: the arrow can be switched off in the mod settings ("View direction arrow").
