An overview map of your whole savegame in a small window. See where you are, spot every resource at a glance and jump anywhere with one click.

Open it with the map button in the top-left corner of the screen.

Features

  • Your map: the terrain, water and towns of your save, with every industry shown by the icon of what it produces. Point at an icon to see the industry's name.
  • Roads, rails and towns: tick Net to draw your streets, railway tracks and town buildings onto the map. It keeps itself up to date as you build or bulldoze, redraws only what changed, and costs nothing while switched off.
  • High visibility: with Net on, tick Viz for bright, opaque colours (yellow streets, magenta tracks, orange towns) that stand out on any terrain.
  • Industry filter: with Ind on, tick Filter to open the filter window. Search by industry or cargo (try "grain"), see every industry type on the map with its input and output cargos and how many there are, and tick the types you want. Show all clears it. The filter stays active when you close the window.
  • Shift+click to spotlight: hold Shift and click a resource to spotlight it: only its type stays on the map. Shift+click again to show all. Holding Shift while pointing at an icon fades the other types as a preview.
  • Camera box: shows where the camera is looking and follows it as you pan and zoom, with an arrow that points the way the camera faces.
  • Click to jump: click anywhere on the map to move the camera there.
  • Small or large: the magnifier button in the header switches sizes. Pin the window to keep it open while you play.
  • Remembers its position, size, pin, the Ind, Net and Viz toggles and the industry filter, also after loading a save. A pinned minimap opens again by itself.
  • The map updates on its own when the terrain, towns or industries change.

Settings

  • Top bar button (default Icon): the button that opens the minimap. Icon: a map icon on a dark tile, like the game's data layers button. Text: the word "Map".
  • View direction arrow (default On): the arrow in the camera box.
  • Debug: performance log (default Off): writes timing lines to the game's log file every 30 s. Only for tracking down slowdowns.

Settings can be changed whenever you start or load a game.

Notes

  • Reads the game state only and never changes your save. Safe to add to or remove from an existing game.
  • The remembered window settings are stored in the savegame, so each game keeps its own. Save the game to keep changes.

  • Changed: the S/L size button is now a magnifier icon in the window header, and the header no longer shows a "Map" title.
  • Changed: the view direction arrow is now a slim white triangle.
