Play in Oregon: 357 real towns from Portland to Eugene, real streets for your bus stops, and real names for train stations and airports.

Pick the name set "Oregon (Towns & Streets)" for a new game. Then just build: every new Road Stop and station in a known city gets a real local name a moment after you place it.

Features

  • Oregon towns: a name set with 357 real towns and places, from big cities to small farm, timber and coast towns (US Census Bureau data).
  • Towns that suit your map: a new game picks its towns by the map's climate. Temperate maps: mostly western Oregon, plus the coast and eastern Oregon. Dry maps: mostly eastern Oregon, plus the mountains. Subarctic maps: mostly the mountains, plus eastern Oregon. Tropical maps: the coast and the southern valleys. Climates from other mods use the whole state.
  • Real street names: new Road Stops in 12 cities (Portland, Eugene, Salem, …) are named after their real streets, like Burnside Street or Willamette Street: 342 streets in all, never twice in the same town.
  • Oregon-style streets everywhere else: other towns draw their stop names from 162 street names in the local style (firs, filberts, quadrant avenues, Cascade peaks, rivers and counties).
  • Real station names: new train stations and airports in 44 cities get real names, like Union Station, Pioneer Square North or Mahlon Sweet Field (40 rail stations, 41 airports).
  • Neighbourhood names: new bus and tram stations in 4 cities are named after real neighbourhoods (95 in all).
  • Everything else follows: industries, depots and their stations are named after your Oregon towns by the game itself.

Settings

  • Real street names (default On): renames new Road Stops in known cities after their real streets.
  • Real station names (default On): renames new train stations, bus and tram stations, airports and harbours in known cities.

Notes

  • Unlike a plain name set, this mod also renames the stops and stations you build after real streets, stations and neighbourhoods in their city.
  • Street and station names also work with any other name set, in towns that carry an Oregon city's name (rename a town to Portland and its next stop is on a real Portland street).
  • Only renames what you build, right after you build it. Existing stops and stations are never touched, and neither are your own renames.
  • Cosmetic only, safe to add to an existing save. A game started with the "Oregon (Towns & Streets)" name set needs this mod to load; in any other save, removing it keeps the names already given and stops renaming.
  • Not affiliated with the "Oregon Names" mod; all lists here are our own.
  • Streets cover 12 cities (Portland, Eugene, Salem, Gresham, Hillsboro, Beaverton, Bend, Medford, Corvallis, Albany, Astoria and Ashland); every other town uses the generic Oregon pool.
