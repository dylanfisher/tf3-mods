Play in Alaska: 282 real towns from Anchorage to Fairbanks, real streets for your bus stops, and real names for train stations, airports and ferry terminals.

Pick the name set "Alaska (Towns & Streets)" for a new game. Then just build: every new Road Stop and station in a known city gets a real local name a moment after you place it.

Features

  • Alaska towns: a name set with 282 real towns and places, from big cities to fishing towns and remote villages (US Census Bureau data).
  • Towns that suit your map: a new game picks its towns by the map's climate. Temperate maps: mostly the Panhandle, plus Southcentral and the islands (Kodiak, Aleutians). Dry maps: the Bush (Arctic and west) and the Interior. Subarctic maps: mostly the Interior, plus Southcentral and the Bush (Arctic and west). Tropical maps and climates from other mods use the whole state.
  • Real street names: new Road Stops in 9 cities (Anchorage, Fairbanks, Juneau, …) are named after their real streets, like 4th Avenue or Cushman Street: 250 streets in all, never twice in the same town.
  • Alaska-style streets everywhere else: other towns draw their stop names from 128 street names in the local style (glaciers, rivers, salmon, spruce, gold rush and bush flying).
  • Real station names: new train stations, airports and ferry terminals in 32 cities get real names, like Denali, Auke Bay Terminal or Merrill Field (3 rail stations, 31 airports, 14 ferry terminals).
  • Neighbourhood names: new bus and tram stations in 1 city are named after real neighbourhoods (28 in all).
  • Everything else follows: industries, depots and their stations are named after your Alaska towns by the game itself.

Settings

  • Real street names (default On): renames new Road Stops in known cities after their real streets.
  • Real station names (default On): renames new train stations, bus and tram stations, airports and harbours in known cities.

Notes

  • Unlike a plain name set, this mod also renames the stops and stations you build after real streets, stations and neighbourhoods in their city.
  • Street and station names also work with any other name set, in towns that carry an Alaska city's name (rename a town to Anchorage and its next stop is on a real Anchorage street).
  • Only renames what you build, right after you build it. Existing stops and stations are never touched, and neither are your own renames.
  • Cosmetic only, safe to add to an existing save. Never remove it from a game started with the "Alaska (Towns & Streets)" name set: the save still loads, but the game crashes as soon as it needs a new name (building a road, for example). Turning the mod back on fixes the save. In any other save, removing it keeps the names already given and stops renaming.
  • Alaska has fewer street-name cities than the other states in this series: real streets for 9 towns (Anchorage, Fairbanks, Juneau, Ketchikan, Sitka, Wasilla, Kodiak, Palmer and Homer). Every other town uses the Alaska-style street list.
  • Not affiliated with the "Alaskan Names" mod; all lists here are our own.
