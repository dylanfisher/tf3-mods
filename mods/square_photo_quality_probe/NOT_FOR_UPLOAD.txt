DEV ONLY - NOT FOR UPLOAD

square_photo_quality_probe is a developer tool for a possible photo mode mod. Never publish it to mod.io
(Mod Hub > My Mods would offer it because `make link` puts it in the staging area).

The top-left "SQPQ boost off" button switches the player's graphics settings to a boosted preset and back
(api.util.setAppConfig, never saved by the probe). With "Restore at pause menu: No" the boost is left on at exit,
so the game may save it into the real settings: note your graphics settings before testing.
The "SQPQ tests" button opens the runs E test window (time of day, pause, environment...); every test puts back
what it changed, also when the pause menu opens, but an autosave during a test can keep the change in the save:
use a throwaway save.
How to run it: plans/square_photo_quality_probe/TESTING.md.

When done testing: disable it in the game's mod list, or `rm` its symlink from staging_area.
