When you build or edit a line, the game switches on passenger loading at every stop whose station can take passengers, including stations that only serve a factory, mine or other industry. With this mod, the line editor leaves passengers off at those stops.

Features

  • No passengers at industry stops: passenger loading stays off at stops whose station has an industry in its catchment area and no homes or workplaces in reach. It works from the very first stop you click.
  • Docks on factory lines too: on a line with such an industry stop, passenger loading also stays off at stops with no homes in reach, such as a dock or another cargo transfer point. A dock on a line without a factory stop is not touched.
  • Stations that also reach houses (for example a town station next to a factory) keep passenger loading.
  • Only the passenger flag is changed. Cargo loading, max load and waiting settings stay as the game set them.
  • Lines with hand-made cargo filters are never changed, just like the game's own automatic settings.

Notes

  • Existing lines are not changed until you edit their route in the line editor, when the game recomputes their automatic settings.
  • Only patches the line editor and stores nothing in your save. Removing it just brings back the game's default passenger loading for lines you edit afterwards.
