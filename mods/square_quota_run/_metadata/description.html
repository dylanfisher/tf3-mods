Quota Run turns a new free game into a roguelike run. Every round your company must move an escalating quota of goods and passengers, and between rounds you draft cards that reshape the economy.

Features

  • Rounds and quotas: each round sets a delivery quota of goods and passengers that grows every round.
  • Card draft: when a round ends, the game pauses and you pick 1 of 3 cards: boons, or boons tied to a curse.
  • Run over: miss a quota or stay in the red for too long and the run ends. You can then keep playing the map as a sandbox.

Notes

  • Only activates in new free games started with the mod enabled. Existing savegames, missions and the campaign are left untouched.
  • Do not add this mod to, or remove it from, an existing savegame.
