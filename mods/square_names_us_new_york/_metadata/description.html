Play in New York: 541 real towns from New York to Buffalo, real streets for your bus stops, and real names for train stations, airports and harbours.

Pick the name set "New York (Towns & Streets)" for a new game. Then just build: every new Road Stop and station in a known city gets a real local name a moment after you place it.

Features

  • New York towns: a name set with 541 real towns and places, from big cities to small canal, mountain, lake and beach towns (US Census Bureau data).
  • Towns that suit your map: a new game picks its towns by the map's climate. Temperate maps: mostly upstate New York, plus downstate (NYC, Hudson Valley) and Long Island. Subarctic maps: the North Country (Adirondacks). Tropical maps: mostly Long Island, plus downstate (NYC, Hudson Valley). Dry maps and climates from other mods use the whole state.
  • Real street names: new Road Stops in 13 cities (New York, Buffalo, Yonkers, …) are named after their real streets, like Broadway or Delaware Avenue: 381 streets in all, never twice in the same town.
  • New York-style streets everywhere else: other towns draw their stop names from 129 street names in the local style (village greens, canal towns, maples, orchards, lakes and the Long Island shore).
  • Real station names: new train stations, airports and harbours in 48 cities get real names, like Grand Central, Penn Station or LaGuardia Airport (52 rail stations, 39 airports, 11 harbours).
  • Neighbourhood names: new bus and tram stations in 5 cities are named after real neighbourhoods (177 in all).
  • Everything else follows: industries, depots and their stations are named after your New York towns by the game itself.

Settings

  • Real street names (default On): renames new Road Stops in known cities after their real streets.
  • Real station names (default On): renames new train stations, bus and tram stations, airports and harbours in known cities.

Notes

  • Street and station names also work with any other name set, in towns that carry a New York city's name (rename a town to New York and its next stop is on a real New York street).
  • Only renames what you build, right after you build it. Existing stops and stations are never touched, and neither are your own renames.
  • Cosmetic only, safe to add to an existing save. A game started with the "New York (Towns & Streets)" name set needs this mod to load; in any other save, removing it keeps the names already given and stops renaming.
  • Not affiliated with the "New York Names" mod; all lists here are our own.
