square_sqperf_all is the SQPERF master switch (plans/sqperf/README.md "Master switch"). It has no
scripts and no params. The engine's mod params table (api.engine.config.getModParams(), runFn's
allModParams) has an entry for every active mod, even one without params, and every SQPERF mod's
debug gate (sqperf.debugOn in tools/sqperf/sqperf.lua) is: its own "Debug: performance log" param
On, OR an entry "square_sqperf_all" in that table. So enabling this mod turns on level 3 (30 s
summaries) and every debug-gated log line of every SQPERF mod at once, without touching their
settings.

Never publish it to mod.io (Mod Hub > My Mods would offer it because `make link` puts it in the
staging area). When done: disable it in the game's mod list (quit to the main menu first before
removing its staging link; see docs/findings.md).
