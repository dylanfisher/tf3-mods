Smart lanes + Smart lights. Roundabouts, slip-lane junctions, crossroads and T-junctions get lanes that fit them right after you build them, and lit crossroads and T-junctions can get an improved 4-phase light plan: one long green per road with every lane at once, and a short pedestrian phase before the cross road goes.

Automatic light timing keeps giving the green to the roads where cars are waiting, and Record traffic counts the vehicles at any junction or roundabout, so you can see what a change did.

Tested in game, side by side against the game's own lanes and lights: Smart lights moved 23-26 % more cars a minute through busy lit crossroads than the game's phases (6 of 6 sets), and smart lanes put 16 more cars a minute through two-lane roundabouts.

Features

  • Roundabouts: entry, ring and exit lanes line up one to one, on the game's roundabouts, those from Roundabouts & Interchanges and rings you draw yourself. Wide rings get an exit-only outer lane; small 2-lane rings with town street arms get extra nodes between the arms, so cars change lanes there instead of at the arms.
  • Slip lanes and ramps: where a slip lane or ramp leaves or joins a road, lanes are matched without crossings; through traffic keeps its lanes.
  • Crossroads and T-junctions: turning traffic never cuts across the through lanes, lanes are never squeezed inside the junction, and turns served by a slip lane are left to it.
  • Lane drops and gains: where a road changes width, the curb lane ends or starts without lanes crossing.
  • Bus lanes: the car lanes get smart lanes; bus lane connections and bus priority phases stay as you made them.
  • Free curb turn: at traffic lights the turn toward the curb also gets green in phases where nothing crosses its path (or always, see Settings).
  • Smart lights: select a lit crossroads or T-junction and press Smart lights to plan its phases around its lanes as they are now, including lanes you set by hand. Once applied the button hides and the status shows "√ Smart lanes | √ Smart lights".
  • Re-time: measures a lit junction's traffic for about 3 game minutes, then sets its cycle and green times to fit: more green for the busier road.
  • Keep timing adjusted (Yes / No): re-times the junction by itself over a long game as its queues change, also with the tool closed.
  • Automatic light timing: set it to All lights and every traffic light on the map is kept adjusted, also ones built later; choose No at a junction to leave it out.
  • Record traffic: counts the vehicles through any junction or roundabout for up to 20 game minutes and shows vehicles per minute, live. Up to 20 can record at once; a new recording shows the previous result below it.
  • Smart Lanes tool (Roads, Tools, next to the Crossing Tool): click a junction, a roundabout or a street to select it, Shift+click to add more (up to 60 junctions), then press Smart lanes. Reset brings back the game's lanes and lights and keeps them: the status reads "Game default (locked)".
  • Light settings in game: the light settings can be changed for the current game on the left of the Smart Lanes tool and of the game's Crossing Tool.
  • Welcome on first load: offers to convert the junctions already on the map and to switch all lights to automatic timing. Save first; it asks once per game.
  • No lights at roundabouts and ramps: the game's automatic lights are switched off where traffic should only give way or merge.

Settings

  • Apply automatically to new roads (On / Off, default On): smart lanes right after you build; Off leaves it to the tool.
  • No lights at roundabouts and ramps (On / Off, default On): no game lights at roundabouts, slip lanes and ramps.
  • Free curb turn at traffic lights (Off / When safe / Always, default Always): when the curb turn may go.
  • Light phases (Game default / Smart Lanes, default Game default): Smart Lanes gives every lit crossroads and T-junction it handles the Smart lights plan.
  • Automatic light timing (Junctions I choose / All lights / Off, default Junctions I choose): where Keep timing adjusted runs; Off stops it everywhere, with no traffic counting.
  • Keep timing adjusted: check interval (x0.5 / x1 / x2 / x4, default x1): how often a junction is checked.
  • Debug: performance log (Off / On, default Off): timing lines in the game's log, for tracking down slowdowns.

Tested against the game's lanes

Each junction built twice side by side, once with Smart Lanes and once as the game makes it, each fed by its own towns for 20 game minutes.

  • Lit crossroads, Smart lights vs the game's phases: +23 % and +26 % cars a minute (2 and 3 lanes each way), 6 of 6.
  • Slip-lane T-junction: 13 to 42 km/h, +27 % cars, 9 of 9 (mostly from switching off the game's lights at the slip lanes).
  • 2-lane roundabout: +16 cars a minute (+27 %), 9 of 9.
  • Lit T-junction: +48 % speed, 9 of 9. Lit crossroads lanes: +27 % speed, 14 of 15.
  • Diamond interchange: +55 % speed, 5 of 6.

Notes

  • Pairs well with Roundabouts & Interchanges, a separate mod of mine that builds complete roundabouts and interchanges; Smart Lanes sets up their lanes as soon as they are placed. Each works on its own.
  • Junctions with five or more roads, single-lane rings and rings with a bus lane are left as the game builds them. You can still change any junction with the Crossing Tool.
  • Safe to add to an existing save. Safe to remove: its lane settings are ordinary junction settings and stay in the save.

Revision 9 (2026-10-17)

  • New: Re-time can measure up to 5 junctions at once.
  • Faster: with no junction set to Keep timing adjusted, the light timing does almost nothing in the background, and Smart Lanes reads its saved data less often.
  • Faster with All lights: the map is searched for new lights once an hour of game time instead of every 5 minutes (lights you build are picked up at once), junctions with little traffic are checked less often, and heavy steps are spread over more frames. In tests the worst frame from light timing dropped by about 85%.
  • Smoother: many traffic recordings or Re-times at once no longer cause a short stutter when they take a sample or save a checkpoint.
  • Changed: building a road no longer changes the lanes of a nearby junction it does not connect to (roundabouts are still set up whole).
  • Changed: a junction Smart Lanes does not handle now says why in plain words, for example "not handled: a road has only bus lanes".
  • Changed: a grayed button's reason (for example Re-time with 5 junctions measuring) now also shows in the status text.
