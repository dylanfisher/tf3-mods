Multi-lane roundabouts that drive with lane discipline. Right after you build a roundabout, Smart Lanes sets which lane may go where at each of its junctions, the same settings you could make by hand with the Crossing Tool.

Works for any one-way ring: the game's roundabout, the roundabouts from Roundabouts & Interchanges and rings you draw yourself, in both driving directions.

Features

  • Matched lanes: lanes line up one to one. The kerb entry lane joins the outer ring lane, the next entry lane the next ring lane, and so on; at an exit the outer ring lane leaves into the kerb exit lane, the next ring lane into the next exit lane. Two lanes only merge into one where the road really gets narrower (a 3-lane arm into a 2-lane ring).
  • Exit-only outer lane: on a roundabout whose exits all have two or more lanes, the outer ring lane leaves at the next exit, as on a turbo roundabout. Traffic going further keeps to the inner lanes.
  • On-ramps into a roundabout: where a one-way ramp joins a multi-lane ring at a junction without an exit (the Roundabout Interchange), the ramp keeps the outer ring lane to itself and the circulating traffic in the outer lanes zips into the next lane in (on a 2-lane ring: both lanes zip into the inner lane), so ramp traffic never has to wait for a gap. Only when the next junction has an exit of two or more lanes, so both ring lanes can leave there right after the merge. Town streets joining a roundabout still give way.
  • Wide exits: a 3-lane exit off a 4-lane ring takes the three outer ring lanes; an exit wider than the ring is fed by the innermost ring lane as well.
  • Splitter islands (separate entry and exit roads) are supported. Tram tracks keep the game's own connections.
  • Slip lanes: where a one-way slip lane, splitter leg or ramp leaves or joins a road (the Channelized Junction and the interchange ramps from Roundabouts & Interchanges, the game's interchange slip roads, or your own), lanes are matched one to one with no crossings. Through traffic keeps its lanes and the kerb lane feeds the slip lane. On a road with three or more lanes the kerb lane before an exit is for leaving only (as at crossroads), unless another junction is just behind it; the other lanes go straight. A joining slip lane merges into the kerb lane, or keeps its own lane where the road's extra kerb lane was added just for it or an exit follows soon after.
  • Crossroads and T-junctions: on a road with three or more lanes each way, the kerb lane only turns right (left in left-hand traffic) and the far lane only turns the other way or goes straight, so turning traffic never cuts across the through lanes inside the junction. Straight traffic keeps out of the next road's kerb lane, which stays free for traffic turning in from the side road. Where a slip lane already serves a turn (the corners of the Channelized Junction), that turn is left to the slip lane: the junction's kerb lanes go straight instead of queuing at the traffic lights to turn. On two-lane roads the lanes are shared (kerb lane: turn or straight; far lane: straight or the other turn), and a single lane goes everywhere. Lanes are never squeezed inside the junction: a turn into a 1-lane road comes from one lane only, the other lanes go where the roads have room, and where two roads turn into the same road they take its lanes side by side instead of sharing one. Where the next road has room, a two-lane road's second lane also goes straight, and on roads with three or more lanes the far lane goes straight as well as turning. That extra straight lane is kept only at junctions with traffic lights, where the straight traffic and the cars turning into the same lane move in different phases. Without lights (switched off at the junction) it turns only, so straight traffic and turning traffic never meet in one lane. The same goes where a highway ramp ends at the crossing road (diamond and parclo interchanges), where a divided road crosses a road, and where one-way streets meet two-way streets. Crossroads keep the game's traffic lights.
  • Free kerb turn at traffic lights: at crossroads and T-junctions with traffic lights, the turn towards the kerb (right in right-hand traffic, left in left-hand traffic) also goes in the phases of the lights where nothing crosses its path: no traffic with green drives into a lane nearer the kerb than the one it turns into, and no crosswalk it drives over has green. Turners in the game never give way once their turn is free, so the turn stays red wherever it would cut across green traffic or pedestrians; where green traffic only enters the same lane, the turners merge with it, which in game keeps the junction flowing better than holding the turn. Set together with the junction's lanes, right after you build it and with the Smart Lanes tool; the tool's Reset gives the turn back to the lights. Where a slip lane serves the turn there is nothing to free. Setting "Free kerb turn at traffic lights".
  • Lane drops and gains: where a road changes width without a junction (3 lanes to 2, or 2 to 3), the lanes are matched from the median side. The kerb lane ends and merges into the next lane, and a new kerb lane is fed by the kerb lane, so no lanes cross. For one-way roads Smart Lanes looks for a two-way road nearby to tell the driving side; far from any (a long highway) it uses the driving side it has already seen on this map, and leaves the spot alone until it has seen one. Traffic lights there stay as they are.
  • Smart Lanes tool (Roads, Tools, next to the Crossing Tool): click a junction of a roundabout, or the roundabout itself between its junctions, to see its lane connections, then press Apply to junction or Apply to roundabout to give it smart lanes. Reset brings back the game's own connections. Crossroads and T-junctions work with Apply to junction, and their Reset brings back the game's own lanes. Slip-lane junctions and lane drops work with Apply to junction; their Reset restores the lanes they had before your first Apply there. Click a street between its junctions to select the junctions at both ends of that stretch (and any lane drops between them); Shift+click to follow the street further, straight on through junctions while it keeps its lanes, up to 10 junctions or 1 km (roundabouts are left to Apply to roundabout). Apply to junction and Reset then work on all of them at once. With something selected, Shift+click adds more: a junction (Shift+click it again to take it out), a street's junctions or a roundabout's, up to 60 junctions; roundabout junctions get the same lanes as with Apply to junction there. The tool shows the lane connections of every selected junction at once, and Apply to junction and Reset work on all of them. Works on junctions you built before adding the mod, too.
  • Lane status: the tool shows what lanes the selected junction or roundabout has now: Smart lanes, Game default, Custom lanes (for example set with the Crossing Tool), "Smart lanes on 3 of 6 junctions", or Not handled. Hovering a junction shows the same in the cursor tooltip.

Settings

In the mod's options when you load or start a game (choose Smart Lanes in the mod list). They apply when the game loads.

  • Apply automatically (On / Off, default On): On gives smart lanes, right after you build them, to roundabouts, slip-lane and ramp junctions, crossroads and T-junctions, and points where a road gets narrower or wider. Off leaves everything you build as the game makes it; the Smart Lanes tool still works.
  • No lights at roundabouts and ramps (On / Off, default On): from 1950 the game puts traffic lights on junctions by itself, including roundabout entries and the points where slip lanes and ramps leave or join a road, so traffic stops where it should only give way or merge. With this On, Smart Lanes switches the lights off at those junctions, right after you build them and when you use the Smart Lanes tool (its Reset gives the choice back to the game). Crossroads, T-junctions, lane drops, and the crossing of a road through a roundabout's centre keep the game's lights. Off: the game decides everywhere. You can still set any junction's lights by hand.
  • Free kerb turn at traffic lights (Off / When safe / Always, default Always): at crossroads and T-junctions with traffic lights, the kerb turn (right in right-hand traffic, left in left-hand traffic) gets green in more phases. When safe: in every phase where nothing with green crosses its path: no other road's traffic into a lane nearer the kerb than the one it turns into, and no green crosswalk on the road it comes from or the road it turns into. Where the lights let a straight road go into the turn's own lane, the turn goes too and merges with that traffic. Always: free in every phase, even across green traffic and crosswalks (turning vehicles do not give way). Set when Smart Lanes handles the junction: right after you build it (also when the game adds its lights a moment later) and when you use the Smart Lanes tool there. The tool's Reset locks the turn again with its road, as the game has it. Off: the junction keeps the game's phases. Junctions already set stay so until you use the tool's Reset (then Apply to junction for the lanes). You can still edit any junction's phases by hand in its Crossing window.
  • Light phases (Game default / Smart Lanes, default Game default): Game default keeps the game's own traffic light phases at crossroads and T-junctions (they only follow the new lanes). Smart Lanes plans the phases of every lit crossroads and T-junction it handles from its lanes: only movements that neither cross nor merge get green together, so turns across oncoming traffic get their own green (a turn arrow, or their road's own phase where one lane turns and goes straight); kerb turns also go where they only merge into green traffic, as with Free kerb turn When safe; pedestrians cross beside parallel traffic or in their own phase, and a short all-red (2 to 4 s) follows every phase. Green times follow the game's (about 19 s for one lane, 23 s for two, 27 s for three), every green phase can end early or be skipped when nobody waits (the all-red phases always run), and a cycle stays within about 90 s, a little longer at big crossroads with crosswalks. Set when Smart Lanes handles the junction: right after you build it (also when the game adds its lights a moment later) and when you use the Smart Lanes tool there; the free kerb turn is not added on top (the plan already lets kerb turns go wherever When safe would). The tool's Reset gives the junction the game's own phases back. Junctions already set keep their phases when you switch back to Game default, until you use the tool's Reset (with Smart Lanes still chosen) or the Crossing window's Reset to Default. You can still edit any junction's phases by hand in its Crossing window.
  • Debug: performance log (Off / On, default Off): writes timing lines (lines starting with [SQPERF]) to the game's log file every 30 s, plus the road tool's hover diagnostics. Only for tracking down slowdowns; slow calls are logged briefly either way.

Companion mod: Roundabouts & Interchanges

Smart Lanes pairs well with Roundabouts & Interchanges (formerly Better Roundabouts), a separate mod of mine. It builds complete roundabouts in one click (round or oval, 1, 2 or 4 ring lanes, splitter islands, hamburger roundabouts), plus Magic Roundabouts, Channelized Junctions with slip lanes and Roundabout Interchanges, and highway interchanges with one-way ramps: Parclo, Parclo two quadrants and Diamond. Smart Lanes sets up their lanes as soon as they are placed. To get it, view my other mods (click the creator name, square) and subscribe to Roundabouts & Interchanges. The two mods work independently, so you can use either one on its own.

Notes

  • Only junctions you have just built or changed are touched. Roundabouts you built before adding the mod, and junctions elsewhere, are never rewritten. When you add an arm to an existing roundabout, only the new junction gets the rule (with the outer lane allowed to continue, so every exit stays reachable).
  • To reach an exit with fewer lanes than the ring, traffic changes lanes between junctions, as it does with the game's own lane settings.
  • Single-lane rings, junctions with a road through the centre and bus lanes are left as the game builds them.
  • You can still change any junction afterwards with the Crossing Tool.
  • Other mods and tests can mark areas of the map for Smart Lanes (the Stress Test Debug Tool's Showcase does this for its side-by-side comparisons): in an area marked "game default" the automatic pass leaves new junctions as the game builds them (the Smart Lanes tool still works there), and in an area marked for light phases lit crossroads get Smart Lanes phases whatever the Light phases setting. For tests an area can also ask for the "fan" pattern: every lane of a lit crossroads connected to every lane of the other roads, with one green phase per approach; it is never used outside such areas.
  • Junctions with five or more roads, bus lanes, or one-way roads only are left as the game builds them; the Smart Lanes tool only shows their connections. Right-click or Esc clears its selection.
  • Safe to add to an existing save. Safe to remove: the lane settings it made are ordinary junction settings and stay in the save, exactly as if you had made them with the Crossing Tool.

  • New: setting "No lights at roundabouts and ramps" (On by default): roundabout junctions and slip-lane and ramp junctions no longer get the game's automatic traffic lights.
  • New: crossroads and T-junctions get smart lanes too (including ramp ends at the crossing road, divided roads and one-way streets), right after you build them and with the Smart Lanes tool. On roads with three or more lanes the kerb lane is for turning only, so the other lanes go straight without traffic crossing them.
  • New: at crossroads and T-junctions, turns that a slip lane serves are left to the slip lane (the corners of the Channelized Junction), so drivers turning there use the slip lane instead of queuing at the traffic lights. On roads with three or more lanes the far lane now also goes straight, so straight traffic keeps every lane but the kerb turn lane.
  • Changed: where a ramp or slip lane joins a road with three or more lanes and there are not enough lanes for both, Smart Lanes looks along the road first. The ramp keeps its own lane (the road's lanes merge among themselves) where the road's kerb lane was added shortly before or an exit follows soon after; on a long road of that width, right after another junction, or where the road loses a lane soon after, the ramp gives way into the kerb lane.
  • New: lane drops and gains. Where a road narrows or widens (for example 3 lanes to 2), the lanes are matched without crossing: the kerb lane ends into the next lane, a new kerb lane is fed by the kerb lane. Right after you build and with the Smart Lanes tool ("Lane drop" / "Lane gain"); traffic lights there are left alone.
  • New: exit lanes before off-ramps. Where a ramp or slip lane leaves a road with three or more lanes and the road keeps its width, the kerb lane becomes exit-only, so through traffic keeps to the other lanes (as at crossroads). The kerb lane stays shared for leaving and going straight when another junction is less than about 80 m behind the exit, unless that kerb lane was added just for the exit.
  • Changed: lane drops on one-way roads far from any two-way road (highway carriageways) get matched lanes too, using the driving side Smart Lanes has already read from a two-way road on the same map.
  • New: the Smart Lanes tool selects streets. Click a street between its junctions: the junctions at both ends of that stretch (and any lane drops between them) are selected and highlighted ("Street: 2 junctions"), with one lane status for all of them, and Apply to junction or Reset changes them all at once. Shift+click follows the street further: every crossroads, slip-lane junction and lane drop along it, up to 10 junctions or 1 km.
  • New: select several junctions in the Smart Lanes tool. With something selected, Shift+click a junction to add it (again to remove it), or a street or roundabout to add its junctions, up to 60 ("5 junctions selected"). The lane connections of all of them are shown at once, the lane status covers all of them, and Apply to junction or Reset changes them all; roundabout junctions get the lanes Apply to junction gives them. A street's lane connections are shown now too.
  • Faster: the Smart Lanes tool reuses what it already worked out about each junction, so Shift+clicking into a large selection, hovering along long roads and keeping a long street or many junctions selected cost far less; the automatic pass after big builds is quicker too.
  • Smoother: after a big build (a whole grid of crossroads at once, or many builds at high game speed) the automatic pass no longer stalls the game for a moment. It spreads its work over the next frames, a few milliseconds each, and writing the lanes and light phases of a junction costs about half of what it did. The lanes it gives are the same. When the game refuses one junction's write (for example a road rebuilt a moment before), the other junctions written with it are sent again one by one instead of being left with the game's lanes.
  • New: on-ramps into a roundabout (the Roundabout Interchange): at a ring junction where a one-way ramp only joins, the ramp gets the outer ring lane to itself and the circulating traffic in the outer lanes zips into the next lane in, instead of the ramp giving way into the outer lane. Applies when the next junction has an exit of two or more lanes; right after you build and with the Smart Lanes tool.
  • Changed: crossroads and T-junctions no longer squeeze lanes. A turn or straight-on gets at most as many lanes as the road it leads into has (no more 4 lanes turning into 1); the other lanes go straight or take the other turn. Where two roads turn into the same road and it has too few lanes for both, the turn with more lanes gives some up to the other directions first, so each lane of the road is fed by one lane. Only where a road has more lanes than all the roads it leads to together do lanes merge, spread as evenly as possible.
  • Changed: at crossroads and T-junctions without traffic lights, the extra straight lane (a two-lane road's second lane, the far lane of a wider road) turns only where straight traffic would otherwise share a lane of the next road with cars turning into it. With traffic lights it still goes straight too. If you switch a junction's lights on or off later, its lanes are not changed by themselves: the Smart Lanes tool then shows them as Custom, and Apply to junction gives it the lanes for its lights.
  • Fixed: a crash when Smart Lanes changed the lanes of a junction that has traffic lights. The lights' phases now follow the new lanes.
  • New: setting "Free kerb turn at traffic lights" (Off / When safe / Always, default Always): at crossroads and T-junctions with traffic lights, the kerb turn (right in right-hand traffic) also gets green in the phases where no green traffic crosses its path (into a lane nearer the kerb) and no crosswalk it drives over has green; where green traffic only enters the same lane, the turn goes too and merges with it. Always (the default) frees it in every phase (turners do not give way). Set with the junction's lanes, right after you build and with the Smart Lanes tool; the tool's Reset locks the turn again.
  • New: setting "Light phases" (Game default / Smart Lanes, default Game default): with Smart Lanes, lit crossroads and T-junctions get planned traffic light phases with their lanes: protected turns (no turn against oncoming traffic; kerb turns also go where they only merge into green traffic, as When safe), pedestrians beside parallel traffic or in their own phase, a short all-red after every phase, green times like the game's. Right after you build and with the Smart Lanes tool; the tool's Reset gives the game's phases back.
  • New: other mods and tests can mark map areas for Smart Lanes: "game default" areas are left alone by the automatic pass, "light phases" areas get Smart Lanes phases at lit crossroads whatever the setting. The Stress Test Debug Tool's Showcase uses this for its side-by-side twins.
  • New (for tests): a marked area can ask for the "fan" pattern, to compare it with the others: at lit crossroads and T-junctions there, every incoming lane connects to every outgoing lane of the other roads (no U-turns), and the lights give each approach its own green phase (plus turns that cross nothing of it), a pedestrian phase and a short all-red after every phase. Junctions without lights there get the normal smart lanes. Not used anywhere else.
  • Fixed: the Smart Lanes tool's Reset at a slip-lane junction no longer writes back old lanes after one of its roads was rebuilt; Reset is then unavailable instead.
  • Fixed: highway on- and off-ramps that leave the carriageway in line with it (the interchanges of Roundabouts & Interchanges) were left with the game's lanes; they now get matched lanes too.
  • Fixed: where ramps leave or join a two-way road alongside it, traffic could turn across the oncoming lanes into a ramp on the far side, and a joining ramp could turn left across the road. Now only the kerb lane peels off into the ramp on its own side, the other lanes go straight, and a joining ramp merges into its own side only. A ramp that leaves on the far side (a left exit in right-hand traffic) stays reachable from the far lane. Ramp ends that meet a road at about a right angle (diamond and cloverleaf ramp ends) keep their turns both ways, also where the ramp end leans towards one direction.
  • Fixed: a junction of three one-way roads that all lead away from it (or all lead into it) stopped Smart Lanes' automatic pass with a script error; such junctions are now left alone, and a junction Smart Lanes cannot handle no longer stops the rest of the build from being handled.
