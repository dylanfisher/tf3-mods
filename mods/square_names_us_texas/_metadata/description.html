Play in Texas: 533 real towns from Houston to San Antonio, real streets for your bus stops, and real names for train stations, airports and ports.

Pick the name set "Texas (Towns & Streets)" for a new game. Then just build: every new Road Stop and station in a known city gets a real local name a moment after you place it.

Features

  • Texas towns: a name set with 533 real towns and places, from big cities to small ranch, oil, border and coast towns (US Census Bureau data).
  • Towns that suit your map: a new game picks its towns by the map's climate. Temperate maps: mostly East and Central Texas, plus the Gulf Coast and the Panhandle and South Plains. Dry maps: mostly West Texas, plus the Panhandle and South Plains. Subarctic maps: the Panhandle and South Plains. Tropical maps: the Gulf Coast and the Rio Grande Valley. Climates from other mods use the whole state.
  • Real street names: new Road Stops in 13 cities (Houston, San Antonio, Dallas, …) are named after their real streets, like Westheimer Road or Congress Avenue: 367 streets in all, never twice in the same town.
  • Texas-style streets everywhere else: other towns draw their stop names from 128 street names in the local style (courthouse squares, live oaks, pecans, mesquite, ranch roads and bayous).
  • Real station names: new train stations, airports and ports in 25 cities get real names, like Union Station, Fort Worth Central or Dallas Love Field (54 rail stations, 28 airports, 9 ports).
  • Neighbourhood names: new bus and tram stations in 4 cities are named after real neighbourhoods (88 in all).
  • Everything else follows: industries, depots and their stations are named after your Texas towns by the game itself.

Settings

  • Real street names (default On): renames new Road Stops in known cities after their real streets.
  • Real station names (default On): renames new train stations, bus and tram stations, airports and harbours in known cities.

Notes

  • Unlike a plain name set, this mod also renames the stops and stations you build after real streets, stations and neighbourhoods in their city.
  • Street and station names also work with any other name set, in towns that carry a Texas city's name (rename a town to Houston and its next stop is on a real Houston street).
  • Only renames what you build, right after you build it. Existing stops and stations are never touched, and neither are your own renames.
  • Cosmetic only, safe to add to an existing save. Never remove it from a game started with the "Texas (Towns & Streets)" name set: the save still loads, but the game crashes as soon as it needs a new name (building a road, for example). Turning the mod back on fixes the save. In any other save, removing it keeps the names already given and stops renaming.
  • Not affiliated with the "Texas Names" mod; all lists here are our own.
