Made a mistake? Bulldoze it right after building and get all of the money back, instead of the vanilla 75 %.

Features

  • Full refund: bulldoze something you built within the refund window and the mod pays out the part of the build cost the game kept back, so you get 100 % back.
  • Covers what you build: stations, depots and other constructions, plus roads and tracks, piece by piece.
  • Fair for edits: branch off a new road or add a platform and what you already paid carries over, so tearing it all down still returns exactly what you spent, never more.
  • Visible in your finances: the top-up is booked as income on the Bulldozer line under construction costs in the Finances window.
  • Longer window: optionally stretch the window from 60 seconds to 2, 5 or 10 minutes.

Settings

  • Refund window (default 60 s): how long after building something bulldozing it still refunds the full cost. Options are 60 s, 2 min, 5 min and 10 min.

Notes

  • The window counts game time, so it stands still while the game is paused. Upgrading or rebuilding something starts its window again.
  • Only what you build while the mod is active is tracked.
  • No effect with infinite money.
  • Changes no game settings and keeps only a short list of recent builds in the savegame. Safe to add to or remove from existing saves.
