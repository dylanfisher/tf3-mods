Line Rate Matcher sizes a line's fleet to the cargo it serves. A logging camp makes 101 logs a year and your ship line moves 66 with 2 ships? One click in the Line Manager shows that 3 ships reach about 99 and 4 about 132, what each option costs, and whether another ship model would carry the logs for less.

Nothing is bought or sold until you press Apply.

Features

  • Toolbar button left of the Auto-Rename (A+) button of a single selected line in the Line Manager. It opens a window titled with the line's name.
  • Line stats: Rate, vehicles, Rate per vehicle and running costs per year, the same numbers the Line Manager and the vehicle windows show.
  • Supply: for each cargo the line loads, what the producers in reach of its loading stops can make per year, what they actually produced and how much other lines already take there. The target follows the cargo that needs the most vehicles.
  • Target: suggested from the supply, or type your own Rate per year, with - and + buttons and a Suggested button to go back.
  • Options table: vehicle counts around the target with the change, the estimated Rate, the price (new vehicles minus what the leaving ones sell for), the yearly cost (running costs plus purchase prices spread over the lifespan) and the cost per unit carried. The count closest to the target is marked "suggested".
  • Compare models: the line's model next to up to 5 other buyable models of the same kind that carry the cargo, each with the count it needs and its costs. The lowest cost per unit is marked "best". Click a model to see its options.
  • Apply: buys copies of the line's vehicle straight onto this line, sends the newest vehicles to the depot to be sold on arrival, or switches the whole fleet to another model (replaced in place like the game's Replace window, plus extra vehicles bought or surplus ones sold). The line above the buttons says what Apply will do, and the window re-reads the line afterwards.
  • Replace window: opens the game's own Replace Vehicles window for the line's vehicles, if you prefer to pick there.

How to use

  • Open the Line Manager and select one line.
  • Click the Line Rate Matcher button (a clock with a cargo box) left of A+.
  • Check the target, pick a row in the options table or a model in the comparison, and press Apply.

Notes

  • GUI only: nothing is stored in the savegame. Safe to add to or remove from existing saves. Apply only sends the game's own buy, assign, send-to-depot and replace commands, the same ones you could give by hand.
  • Rates for the line's own model scale exactly with the number of vehicles. Rates for other models are estimates from their capacity, speed and loading speed, marked with ≈.
  • Passenger lines get no supply target: type the Rate you want.
  • Trains, multiple units and lines that mix vehicle types get no model comparison; adding and removing copies still works. Lines with mission vehicles can't switch models.
  • Translated into all game languages.
