Clicked or deleted a stop by accident and can't remember which one? Line Editor Undo adds Undo and Redo buttons to the line manager, so you can step back through your edits of a line's stops, put them all back the way they were when you opened the line, and redo what you undid.

Features

  • Undo button (a curved-arrow icon) left of the Auto-Rename button of a single selected line: each click undoes one edit (adding, removing or moving a stop, a waypoint, a terminal, load mode, waiting times or cargo settings), most recent first.
  • Revert all: Shift+click Undo puts every stop back the way it was when you opened the line.
  • Redo button right of Undo: re-applies the edits you undid, one per click, also after a revert all. Making a new edit clears what can be redone.
  • Tooltips name the edit the next click undoes or redoes and how many changes you made since opening the line. Up to 50 steps are kept; Shift+click always reaches the start.
  • Each button stays greyed out while there is nothing to undo or redo.

How to use

  • Open the line manager and select one line. The button row reads Undo, Redo, Auto-Rename, colour, Buy Vehicles.
  • Click Undo to take back your last stop edit. Click again to go further back.
  • Shift+click Undo to revert all changes since you opened the line.
  • Click Redo to bring back an edit you undid.
  • The history starts each time you open a line, and is cleared when you switch to another line or close the line manager.

Notes

  • GUI only: the undo and redo history lives in memory and is never saved. Safe to add to or remove from existing saves.
  • Only stops and the line's stop settings are reverted. Name, colour and vehicles are left alone.
  • If a station from the state you go back to has been demolished, nothing is changed and a message explains why.
