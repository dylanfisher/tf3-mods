Stress Test Debug Tool turns a free game into a dense, busy map in minutes: big towns, lots of vehicles, heavy traffic, fast time. It is made for performance-testing mods (and the game) under load.

Warning: this tool changes your save (towns, vehicles, money, date). Use a copy of a save, for testing only. Never use it on a save you care about.

Features

A button in the top-left button row (a gauge icon, or the word "Debug") opens the window. Each section works like a form: pick the options, then press its green button. Actions run one after another from a queue (shown at the top of the window, with "Clear queue" to drop what is waiting); pressing a button again while its batch is still queued or running does nothing. Every action writes an [SQST] line to the window's log and to stdout.txt.

  • Game: add 10M or 100M, keep the balance above 50M (checked every 2 s), jump to any year from 1850 to 2150, set the game speed up to 16x, and hide all notifications (on by default).
  • Game: town ratings: make every town ignore noise and pollution (sets their rating sensitivity to zero, as the town editor does), or restore the default sensitivities.
  • Towns: grow all towns or the one nearest the camera (x2 to x100) with 1 to 10 build-out passes for denser towns; spawn 1 to 25 new towns (Small to Huge) near the camera or spread over the map, optionally connected by road to their nearest town; freeze or unfreeze town development; a readout of towns, capacity and buildings.
  • Towns: test grid: identical towns (same seed, size and street angle) at exact, equally spaced positions: 2 x 2, 3 x 3 or a row of 4, 1 to 6 km apart, around the map centre or the camera, optionally linked by road to their neighbours (and diagonals). Streets "Wide grid" first lays a square grid of large 4-lane town streets (400 to 800 m across, by town size) where each town goes, so the town grows along wide roads; "Wide grid, locked" also stops the town from changing or extending those streets, and makes the grid's outer ring a country road, so the town builds no houses outside it. For comparing traffic between towns on a flat map: start a Blank Slate map, remove its towns and roads, then create the grid and freeze the towns.
  • Towns: junction test (with Roundabouts & Interchanges enabled): a grid of test towns with one of each of its junctions in the middle of every grid cell (Roundabout, Magic Roundabout, Channelized X and T, the Roundabout, Dumbbell and Peanut Interchanges, Parclo, Parclo 2 and Diamond; or only the roundabouts or only the interchanges), each arm linked by road to a corner town (highway arms by a highway, both carriageways all the way to the town). No other road joins the towns, so all traffic between them drives through the junctions. Towns 2 to 4 km apart, around the map centre or the camera, on a wide street grid (default) or their own streets; use an empty flat map. "Traffic: Bus lines" then adds two bus lines per junction between its opposite corner towns, so buses drive straight through it (the Crossing test gets four lines between its towns the same way).
  • Towns: showcase (with Roundabouts & Interchanges enabled): one button fills the map with groups: one junction with a medium town on every arm (all towns the same size), close around it, groups not joined to each other. The junctions take turns: every interchange (highway arms reach their towns as highways), every roundabout, and plain game crossroads and T-junctions of ordinary streets (lanes per arm, angle and traffic lights drawn too), each with random settings from a new seed per press, written to the log. Every group is built side by side as identical twins, one with Smart Lanes and one left at the game default (lit plain crossings get a third twin for Smart Lanes light phases), so the two can be compared under the same traffic. With Smart Lanes enabled, the Showcase tells it each twin's zone before building, and Smart Lanes leaves the game-default twin alone and gives the third twin its light phases whatever its own setting; without Smart Lanes every twin stays at the game default. Every town street is locked against town changes (the wide street grid, the town's own first streets and the links to the junction; unused corner stubs are removed), so the towns stay inside their grids: after the links every town grows x10 with its buildings shifted to the higher levels, for a dense centre and no streets or houses outside the grid. Keep balance is switched on and bus lines across every group follow the build. Each group's zone is written to the log for the traffic log. Remove all roads and Remove towns (grid or all towns) clear the zones in Smart Lanes again.
  • Towns: lights showcase: one button fills the map with every lit junction shape: 2x2 X, 3x3 X, 3x2 X (main road 3 lanes each way, side road 2) and a T junction with 2 lanes each way (lanes per direction, two-way country roads), three sets of each. Every junction is at 90 degrees and lined up with the map, with a medium town on each arm (north, east, south, west; a T has three), each linked by one straight large country road (3 lanes each way) into the middle of its grid's facing side. That point is a three-lane roundabout (two lanes where three does not fit) joined to the grid's outer ring and central street, so the town entry never holds up the traffic under test (the same in every twin; before 1940, the grid's crossroads). Where a road meets a road with more lanes, its outer lane feeds all the extra ones and they all lead back into it, so no lane on a link ends blind. The traffic light is asked for at every junction (from 1950 the game also places its own; before 1950 a dialog warns, and a junction without a light gets one). Every set is built three times side by side with identical settings: Smart Lanes, the game default and Smart Lanes with every turn fanned into the exit lanes at the light (game phases; without Smart Lanes all three stay at the game default). As in the Showcase the town streets are locked to their grids; after the links every town grows once with higher building levels to about 400 people (each town from its own capacity), denser and taller, not wider. No bus lines: the towns' own traffic. A new seed per press, Keep balance switched on, all 12 sets, with every twin once in every position per shape for a fair comparison, need a 20 km map (36 junctions, 135 towns; a 16 km map holds 6 sets, positions not balanced); the traffic log follows it like the Showcase, and now also counts the cars through each junction per minute and splits each arm's queue into the side towards and away from the junction.
  • Towns: remove towns: destroy the town nearest the camera, the test towns (of the test grid, the Junction and Crossing tests and both Showcases, found by name and, in the session that made them, by the town itself), or all towns, together with their streets and buildings (the game's own town removal, as the map editor uses it). Asks for confirmation first and cannot be undone.
  • Towns: remove all roads: remove every street on the map, town streets and your own roads (streets that are part of a station, depot or other construction stay; tracks are not touched). Asks for confirmation first and cannot be undone. Towns rebuild their streets while they develop, so remove or freeze the towns first.
  • Traffic: add vehicles: buy 1 to 25 copies of each line's first vehicle for all your lines (at the line's depot, one at a time; stops when the money runs out), and sell the vehicles added this session.
  • Traffic: clear traffic: send every car driver home at once (uses one of the game's debug commands; your own vehicles are never touched). Drivers start new trips after a few seconds, so "Keep traffic cleared" repeats it every 2, 5, 10, 60 or 120 seconds (the longer intervals let traffic build up between clears). "Where" limits it to cars in towns (traffic between towns, for example through the junction test's junctions, keeps driving) or to cars outside towns.
  • Traffic: auto bus lines: for the town nearest the camera, the 5 nearest or all towns (up to 25), 1, 2 or 4 lines per town. Each town first gets a road depot (one of yours nearby, or a new one built and connected to the streets; a town where none fits is skipped with nothing built), then two bus stops 300-800 m apart on its streets, a line between them and 2, 5 or 10 of the newest buses. "Remove added bus lines" sells those buses, waits until they are gone, then deletes the lines made this session (also those of the Junction and Crossing test's Traffic).
  • Traffic: traffic log: every 10, 30 or 60 game seconds (none while paused) writes the traffic of every Showcase junction twin (every car driving on the map is read: cars in the junction and on each of its approach roads out to 200 m beyond it, mean and median speed, standing and queued cars, queued cars per approach, how long they stand), the whole map, every Showcase and Lights showcase bus line (round trips) and the towns' traffic ratings to stdout.txt as [SQST] FLOW lines, for a Smart Lanes / game default comparison of the twins. After a reload the zones come from Smart Lanes (when enabled). It only reads, spread over frames so the game keeps running smoothly; it warns when Keep traffic cleared is on, which would empty the roads. The tool's make flow report (in its source repository) turns the lines into per-pair tables.
  • Traffic: readout: lines, vehicles, sim persons and cars driving at the last clear.
  • Probe tab (opt-in, see Settings): the in-game experiments the tool was built from, for mod developers. Every probe button changes your save.

Settings

Load-time settings in the mod's options when you load or start a game (choose Stress Test in the mod list). They apply when the game loads: to change one, save, quit to the main menu, change it and load again. All start off or at the default, so the game is unchanged until you pick them.

  • Top bar button (Text / Icon, default Icon): the button that opens the window. Icon: a gauge icon on a dark tile, like the game's data layers button. Text: the word "Debug".
  • No costs (Off / On, default Off): building and vehicles cost nothing (the game's own noCosts setting, as in the official No Costs mod).
  • Sandbox mode (Off / On, default Off): the game's sandbox mode with the town editor and industry menus, as in the official Sandbox mod.
  • Vehicles never expire (Off / On, default Off): no vehicle model goes out of production, as in the official "No end year for vehicles" mod.
  • No noise and pollution (Off / On, default Off): vehicles emit no noise or pollution, the game stops updating the emissions of industries, stations, depots and warehouses (existing ones keep their current value, new industries start at zero), and noise and pollution no longer lower town ratings (towns given their own sensitivity in the town editor or the Game tab keep it).
  • Sim person trip recalculation (x1 / x2 / x5, default x1): how often town residents pick new destinations. x1 is the game's default; higher means more trips and more simulation load.
  • Town development interval (Default / Fast / Very fast, default Default): how often towns grow. Default is the game's own interval (60), Fast is 15, Very fast is 5.
  • Car traffic (Default / Drive: destinations / Drive: all town buildings, default Default): experimental, for testing only. Town buildings no longer allow walking or lines (public transport) as a way to travel, so more trips go by car. Drive: destinations changes shops and workplaces; Drive: all town buildings changes homes too. It changes the town buildings in your save as they are built or rebuilt (use Towns, Grow to rebuild a town right away); after you remove the mod they return to normal only when rebuilt. Check the effect with Scan on the Traffic tab (Last trip mode: DRIVE / WALK / LINES).
  • Town building capacity (x1 / x2 / x4, default x1): experimental, for testing only. Multiplies the residents and workers of every town building (not the goods it needs), so more people make more trips, including between towns. Same as Car traffic: it changes buildings as they are built or rebuilt and they return to normal only when rebuilt. x4 can slow the game down a lot.
  • Show probe tab (developers) (Off / On, default Off): adds the Probe tab with the in-game experiments the tool was built from. Each of its buttons changes your save; for mod developers only.

For more car traffic between towns (for example to test roundabouts and interchanges): Car traffic = Drive: destinations and Town building capacity = x2, then Towns, Grow once, and the Junction test's Traffic = Bus lines for guaranteed traffic through every junction.

Notes

  • Hidden notifications and the town rating sensitivities are stored in the save: uncheck "Hide all notifications" and press "Restore defaults" before removing the mod.
  • Stops and depots built by auto bus lines stay when the lines are removed; bulldoze them by hand if needed.
  • Added vehicles and bus lines are remembered for this session only: after a reload the remove buttons no longer know them.
  • All settings take effect when the save is loaded, so changing one needs a reload. "No noise and pollution" stores nothing in the save: turn it off (or remove the mod) and reload, and vehicles, the emissions script and the default town ratings are back to normal.
  • Look for [SQST] lines in stdout.txt (in the game's crash_dump folder) for what each action did and how long it took.

  • Changed: a Showcase junction refused with "Construction Not Possible" is tried once more with freshly drawn settings of the same type; the dialog lists it, the log keeps the refused settings.

  • Changed: a refused placement's log line names what it collided with (nodes, asset groups, fields, ...) instead of "other".
  • Fixed: bus lines between test towns reuse a depot only on the same road network and never buy a bus that can't reach the line (a new depot near the first stop, else the line is given up; a bus that can't join its line is sold again). Depots keep 400 m from the map edge, and a depot whose street connection fails is removed again.

A new seed per press, Keep balance switched on, bus lines always added. Each group is built as side-by-side twins with identical settings (Smart Lanes / game default, plus Smart Lanes light phases at lit plain crossings), and each group's zone and mode go to the log.

Every street around a Showcase junction is locked too, so towns can't grow streets off the crossing. A refused link logs what it collided with.
