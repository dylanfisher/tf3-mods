The industry window shows cargo-flow arrows for one industry at a time. Industry Flows shows them for every industry of the types you pick, all at once, so you can see where to build supply chains without clicking through each industry.

Click the Flows button in the top-left button ridge to open the panel.

Features

  • Search by industry name or by cargo ("fuel" finds the oil refinery). Each type is listed with its input and output cargos and how many are on the map.
  • Select one or more types to draw their arrows on the map.
  • Toggles: Outputs (to the industries and towns that take their cargo), Inputs (from their suppliers) and Potential links.
  • Live arrows: moving arrows carry cargo that was delivered in the last year. Faded, still arrows are possible links with no cargo moving yet.
  • Summary: a line counts the industries, links and active links.

Notes

  • Arrows show while the panel is open and clear when you close it. Clicking an industry or town opens its window as usual; close it to return to your flows.
  • Your search, selection and toggles are kept for the rest of the session.
  • Very busy maps are capped at 400 arrows, active ones first.
  • Reads the game state only: it changes nothing in the simulation, stores nothing in the savegame, and can be added to or removed from existing saves.
