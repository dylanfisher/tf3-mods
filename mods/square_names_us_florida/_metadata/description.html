Play in Florida: 479 real towns from Jacksonville to Miami, real streets for your bus stops, and real names for train stations, airports and ports.

Pick the name set "Florida (Towns & Streets)" for a new game. Then just build: every new Road Stop and station in a known city gets a real local name a moment after you place it.

Features

  • Florida towns: a name set with 479 real towns and places, from big cities to small beach, citrus and Keys towns (US Census Bureau data).
  • Towns that suit your map: a new game picks its towns by the map's climate. Temperate maps: mostly North Florida, plus the Panhandle and Central Florida. Subarctic maps: North Florida and the Panhandle. Tropical maps: mostly South Florida, plus Central Florida and the Keys. Dry maps and climates from other mods use the whole state.
  • Real street names: new Road Stops in 13 cities (Jacksonville, Miami, Tampa, …) are named after their real streets, like Duval Street or Collins Avenue: 336 streets in all, never twice in the same town.
  • Florida-style streets everywhere else: other towns draw their stop names from 137 street names in the local style (palms, citrus, shore birds, bays and counties).
  • Real station names: new train stations, airports and ports in 16 cities get real names, like Vizcaya, Church Street or PortMiami (15 rail stations, 14 airports, 5 ports).
  • Neighbourhood names: new bus and tram stations in 4 cities are named after real neighbourhoods (63 in all).
  • Everything else follows: industries, depots and their stations are named after your Florida towns by the game itself.

Settings

  • Real street names (default On): renames new Road Stops in known cities after their real streets.
  • Real station names (default On): renames new train stations, bus and tram stations, airports and harbours in known cities.

Notes

  • Street and station names also work with any other name set, in towns that carry a Florida city's name (rename a town to Jacksonville and its next stop is on a real Jacksonville street).
  • Only renames what you build, right after you build it. Existing stops and stations are never touched, and neither are your own renames.
  • Cosmetic only, safe to add to an existing save. A game started with the "Florida (Towns & Streets)" name set needs this mod to load; in any other save, removing it keeps the names already given and stops renaming.
  • Not affiliated with the "Florida Names" mod; all lists here are our own.
