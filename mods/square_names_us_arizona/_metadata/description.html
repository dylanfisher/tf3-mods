Play in Arizona: 275 real towns from Phoenix to Tucson, real streets for your bus stops, and real names for train stations and airports.

Pick the name set "Arizona (Towns & Streets)" for a new game. Then just build: every new Road Stop and station in a known city gets a real local name a moment after you place it.

Features

  • Arizona towns: a name set with 275 real towns and places, from big cities to small desert, mining and reservation towns (US Census Bureau data).
  • Towns that suit your map: a new game picks its towns by the map's climate. Temperate maps: mostly the high country, plus the southeast grasslands. Dry maps: mostly the low desert, plus the high country and the southeast grasslands. Subarctic maps: the high country. Tropical maps: the low desert and the Colorado River. Climates from other mods use the whole state.
  • Real street names: new Road Stops in 13 cities (Phoenix, Tucson, Mesa, …) are named after their real streets, like Camelback Road or Speedway Boulevard: 316 streets in all, never twice in the same town.
  • Arizona-style streets everywhere else: other towns draw their stop names from 129 street names in the local style (saguaros, mesquite, mountain ranges, canals and counties).
  • Real station names: new train stations and airports in 14 cities get real names, like Sky Harbor Airport, Williams Depot or Falcon Field (3 rail stations, 14 airports).
  • Neighbourhood names: new bus and tram stations in 3 cities are named after real neighbourhoods (38 in all).
  • Everything else follows: industries, depots and their stations are named after your Arizona towns by the game itself.

Settings

  • Real street names (default On): renames new Road Stops in known cities after their real streets.
  • Real station names (default On): renames new train stations, bus and tram stations, airports and harbours in known cities.

Notes

  • Street and station names also work with any other name set, in towns that carry an Arizona city's name (rename a town to Phoenix and its next stop is on a real Phoenix street).
  • Only renames what you build, right after you build it. Existing stops and stations are never touched, and neither are your own renames.
  • Cosmetic only, safe to add to an existing save. A game started with the "Arizona (Towns & Streets)" name set needs this mod to load; in any other save, removing it keeps the names already given and stops renaming.
  • Not affiliated with the "Arizona Names" mod; all lists here are our own.
