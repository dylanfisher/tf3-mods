See where passengers are really going. The station window and the person window get a small Passenger destinations section that draws the game's moving cargo arrows to the homes, shops and workplaces they are travelling to.

If you liked the arrows on your industries, good news: there are now about thirty more per bus stop.

Features

  • Station window: a single row above Terminals. Tick All or single terminal numbers. Arrows go from the station to the areas its waiting passengers are heading for (nearby destinations are grouped, busiest first). A summary under the row counts them per town and splits them into Home, Shop and Work.
  • Person window: tick Show destination (under Current Route). One arrow from the person, or their stop or vehicle, to the building they are going to, with a note such as "Riding Line 3, off at Main Street -> Work, Springfield".
  • Next stop: tick Next stop (station) or Next stop instead of final destination (person) to see the stop where they leave the current line instead.
  • Live: arrows and counts refresh every second while a toggle is on.

Notes

  • Arrows show while a toggle is on and clear when you untick it or close the window. At most 30 arrows are drawn per station; the rest are only counted.
  • Workplaces in industries count as "Other" in the town list.
  • Reads the game state only: it changes nothing in the simulation, stores nothing in the savegame, and can be added to or removed from existing saves.
