Play in Washington: 520 real towns from Seattle to Spokane, real streets for your bus stops, and real names for train stations, airports and ferry terminals.

Pick the name set "Washington (Towns & Streets)" for a new game. Then just build: every new Road Stop and station in a known city gets a real local name a moment after you place it.

Features

  • Washington towns: a name set with 520 real towns and places, from big cities to small farm and harbour towns (US Census Bureau data).
  • Towns that suit your map: a new game picks its towns by the map's climate. Temperate maps: mostly western Washington, plus the coast and eastern Washington. Dry maps: mostly eastern Washington, plus the mountains. Subarctic maps: the mountains and the north. Tropical maps: the coast and the islands. Climates from other mods use the whole state.
  • Real street names: new Road Stops in 24 cities (Seattle, Spokane, Tacoma, …) are named after their real streets, like Pike Street or Pacific Avenue: 374 streets in all, never twice in the same town.
  • Washington-style streets everywhere else: other towns draw their stop names from 132 street names in the local style (numbered avenues, trees, mountains, rivers and counties).
  • Real station names: new train stations, airports and ferry terminals in 18 cities get real names, like King Street, Paine Field or Colman Dock (33 rail stations, 12 airports, 7 ferry terminals).
  • Neighbourhood names: new bus and tram stations in 13 cities are named after real neighbourhoods (118 in all).
  • Everything else follows: industries, depots and their stations are named after your Washington towns by the game itself.

Settings

  • Real street names (default On): renames new Road Stops in known cities after their real streets.
  • Real station names (default On): renames new train stations, bus and tram stations, airports and harbours in known cities.

Notes

  • Unlike a plain name set, this mod also renames the stops and stations you build after real streets, stations and neighbourhoods in their city.
  • Street and station names also work with any other name set, in towns that carry a Washington city's name (rename a town to Seattle and its next stop is on a real Seattle street).
  • Only renames what you build, right after you build it. Existing stops and stations are never touched, and neither are your own renames.
  • Cosmetic only, safe to add to an existing save. Never remove it from a game started with the "Washington (Towns & Streets)" name set: the save still loads, but the game crashes as soon as it needs a new name (building a road, for example). Turning the mod back on fixes the save. In any other save, removing it keeps the names already given and stops renaming.
  • Not affiliated with the "Washington Names" mod; all lists here are our own.
