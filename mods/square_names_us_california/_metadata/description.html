Play in California: 550 real towns from Los Angeles to San Diego, real streets for your bus stops, and real names for train stations, airports and ports.

Pick the name set "California (Towns & Streets)" for a new game. Then just build: every new Road Stop and station in a known city gets a real local name a moment after you place it.

Features

  • California towns: a name set with 550 real towns and places, from big cities to small mountain, desert, farm and beach towns (US Census Bureau data).
  • Towns that suit your map: a new game picks its towns by the map's climate. Temperate maps: mostly the Bay Area, plus the Central Coast, the North Coast and far north and the Central Valley. Dry maps: mostly the deserts, plus the Central Valley and Southern California. Subarctic maps: mostly the Sierra Nevada and the high mountains, plus the North Coast and far north. Tropical maps: mostly Southern California, plus the Central Coast. Climates from other mods use the whole state.
  • Real street names: new Road Stops in 13 cities (Los Angeles, San Diego, San Jose, …) are named after their real streets, like Sunset Boulevard or Market Street: 360 streets in all, never twice in the same town.
  • California-style streets everywhere else: other towns draw their stop names from 127 street names in the local style (missions, oaks, palms, redwoods, coast, orchards and counties).
  • Real station names: new train stations, airports and ports in 17 cities get real names, like Union Station, Santa Fe Depot or the Ferry Building (79 rail stations, 17 airports, 9 ports).
  • Neighbourhood names: new bus and tram stations in 4 cities are named after real neighbourhoods (92 in all).
  • Everything else follows: industries, depots and their stations are named after your California towns by the game itself.

Settings

  • Real street names (default On): renames new Road Stops in known cities after their real streets.
  • Real station names (default On): renames new train stations, bus and tram stations, airports and harbours in known cities.

Notes

  • Unlike a plain name set, this mod also renames the stops and stations you build after real streets, stations and neighbourhoods in their city.
  • Street and station names also work with any other name set, in towns that carry a California city's name (rename a town to Los Angeles and its next stop is on a real Los Angeles street).
  • Only renames what you build, right after you build it. Existing stops and stations are never touched, and neither are your own renames.
  • Cosmetic only, safe to add to an existing save. A game started with the "California (Towns & Streets)" name set needs this mod to load; in any other save, removing it keeps the names already given and stops renaming.
  • Not affiliated with the "California Names" mod; all lists here are our own.
