Placing a bus or tram stop and can't see where it's needed? Better Bus Overlay switches the map to a line overview while you place a street stop or a bus/tram station: every bus, tram and truck line is drawn in its colour, town buildings are tinted by type with the ones your lines don't serve yet standing out, and every stop shows how many passengers are waiting. The Bus Lane Tool and the Crosswalk Tool show the same line routes, so you can see which roads your buses use.

Features

  • Line routes: all your bus, tram and truck lines are drawn on the map in their line colours, with their stops marked. Train, air and ship lines are left out.
  • Building types: town buildings are coloured residential (green), commercial (blue) or industrial (yellow), in the game's district colours.
  • Service gaps: buildings that nobody travels to or from on your lines keep the full colour. Buildings your lines already serve are nearly grey with only a hint of their colour, so the gaps stand out.
  • Waiting passengers: each stop and station shows how many passengers are waiting, in the game's usual blue tag. The number turns yellow when some of them are unhappy and red when more than half are.
  • Unused stops: stops and stations that no line passes through yet show a dark "No line" tag instead of the bus icon, while you place stops and in the Lines window, also while you edit a line.
  • Busy stops: in the Lines window, each stop's pin is coloured by how many passengers wait there, on a smooth scale from light blue (quiet) through yellow (about 40 waiting) to red (80 or more).
  • Bus lane and crosswalk tools: while the Bus Lane Tool or the Crosswalk Tool is selected, your bus, tram and truck lines are drawn too, so you can see which roads need bus lanes and where crossings would slow your buses. Building colours and stop tags stay off there.
  • Works for street bus and tram stops and for bus/tram stations, also in underground mode.

Notes

  • "Served" means the game's passengers actually ride your lines to or from the building (as in the Passenger Flow layer), not just that a stop is nearby. A new stop shows up as served once people start using it.
  • A data layer you pick yourself (Data Layers button) still replaces the building colours, as in the base game. The line routes stay.
  • Truck stations, depots, train stations, airports and harbours keep the default view.
  • GUI only: nothing is stored in the savegame. Safe to add to or remove from existing saves.
