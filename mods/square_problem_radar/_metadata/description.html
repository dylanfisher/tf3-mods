Problem Radar collects the problems in your transport network into one compact, sortable window, and stays quiet while everything runs.

Features

  • Vehicles: blocked, no path (with the missing piece where the game reports it), stuck (standing en route past a threshold per carrier; stops, harbour and runway queues and user-stopped vehicles are left out), trains waiting behind another train.
  • Lines: broken (no path, too few or incompatible stops), no vehicles, cargo issues, overloaded (more waiting at a stop than several vehicle visits can carry), underused (low utilisation), bunching (vehicles clumped together or long gaps).
  • Stations: overcrowded, stop unusable for the line's vehicles.
  • Fleet: worn, past its lifespan, idle in a depot.
  • Economy: lines that lost money over the last 12 months (in at least 3 of the 4 quarters), and lines whose average utilisation over about a year is very low. Bigger losses and emptier lines get a higher severity.
  • Badge: a small badge or a compact one-row panel in the top-left button ridge shows the number of problems, coloured by the worst severity.
  • Tabs for All, Vehicles, Lines, Stations, Fleet and Economy, each with its count. Click a row to move the camera there and open the vehicle, line or station window.
  • Notifications: each alert type can be set to Off, List (shown in the radar only) or Notify (also a game notification when the problem first appears). Notifications are rate-limited and can be paused.
  • Snooze: the Zz button on a row hides a problem you already know about (say, a line you accept losing money) for 1 month to 5 years of game time. It is not counted and sends no notifications while snoozed. The Snoozed tab lists it with its end date and can wake it early.
  • Smoothing: a problem is listed once it has lasted 30 game seconds and stays until it has been gone that long, so alerts don't flicker (adjustable, 0 = instant).

Settings

  • All settings are in the radar's Settings tab and saved per savegame: alert types, thresholds (stuck minutes, line limits, worn condition, depot days, money and utilisation limits) and smoothing.

Notes

  • Economy severity combines how little of its vehicle costs a line earns back with how big the loss is next to all your lines' costs. Utilisation is tracked by the mod itself, so low-use rows appear after about half a game year.
  • Reads the game state only: it never changes vehicles, lines or money. Safe to add to an existing save.
