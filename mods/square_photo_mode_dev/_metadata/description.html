DEV ONLY - NOT FOR UPLOAD. Never publish this mod to mod.io. It is a developer switch for Photo Mode and does nothing useful for players.

While it is enabled, the Camera tab's Orbit right after a jump plays an orbit cutscene attached to that subject (a vehicle's carriage or a model entity; round 13), else, with the view closer than 450 m, around the static anchor nearest the view's centre (round 16), and Start on the Tour tab in Aerials plays a chain of cutscene flights whose keys are attached to anchors Photo Mode picks over places of interest, turning away from the map edge (round 15 / 16; its keyframe files are in this mod). Both write what they found to stdout.txt. It has no scripts and no settings.
