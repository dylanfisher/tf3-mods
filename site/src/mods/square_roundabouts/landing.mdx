One click, one complete roundabout: the ring, the approach roads and, if you like, splitter islands, a road or tram
line through the middle and a planted center island. Six more menu entries build a **Magic Roundabout**, a
**channelized junction**, a **roundabout interchange** and three **highway interchanges**. Once placed, it's all
ordinary road you can edit, extend and connect.

## What you can build

- **Roundabouts**: round or oval, from a 10 m mini to 60 m, 1, 2 or 4 ring lanes, three to six arms or none,
  either direction. Splitter islands, a road or tram line through the center, grass, trees, a fountain or paving.
- **Magic Roundabout**: five minis on a two-way ring, like Swindon's.
- **Channelized Junction**: a T or X with slip lanes across the corners.
- **Roundabout Interchange**: a ring over or under a highway, as two bridges, a dumbbell or a peanut.
- **Parclo and Diamond**: highway interchanges in three sizes and three ramp lengths.

Right- or left-hand traffic, steel bridges before 1970 and concrete after, all from 1940, only base-game roads and
trees.

## Using it

The entries sit in the road menu next to the game's own roundabout and interchanges. Pick your settings in the side
panel, place, and connect your roads to the loose arm ends. It runs nothing while you play, so it's safe in any
save. Pairs well with **Smart Lanes** (lane rules on busy rings) and **Bulk Bulldozer**.
