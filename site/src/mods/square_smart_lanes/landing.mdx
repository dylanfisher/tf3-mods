Lane discipline at every junction you build. Place a roundabout, a slip lane, a crossroads or a T-junction and Smart
Lanes sets which lane goes where, the way you could by hand with the game's Crossing Tool. Where traffic should only
give way or merge, it switches off the traffic lights the game adds on its own.

## What it does

- **Roundabouts**: each entry lane joins its own ring lane and leaves into its own exit lane. Small two-lane town
  rings get a spot between the arms where cars change lanes.
- **Slip lanes and ramps**: no lane crossings; through traffic keeps its lanes.
- **Crossroads and T-junctions**: on wide roads the curb lane only turns, so turning cars never cut across.
- **Traffic lights**: none at roundabout entries and slip lanes, and optional planned light phases.
- **Lane drops and bus lanes**: matched without crossings; bus lanes are left as you set them.

## Using it

It's on by default: build a junction and it gets smart lanes. Junctions elsewhere are never touched. To change one,
open **Roads, Tools, Smart Lanes** next to the Crossing Tool, click a junction or a street (Shift+click for up to
60), and press Apply, or Reset for the game's own lanes. Safe to add to an existing save and safe to remove.

## Does it help?

Every junction was built twice on test maps, once with the game's lanes and once with Smart Lanes, and measured for
20 game minutes. Smart Lanes won 46 of 48 pairs, and was three times as fast through slip-lane junctions. The pages
below show every twin side by side.
