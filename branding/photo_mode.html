Photo Mode: launch kit

Nine gallery slides and a share poster in Photo Mode's own postcard style (branding/postcard.js): real pictures taken with the mod as big, slightly tilted postcards, each with the window that made it taped beside it. The slides live in shots.js like every other mod's; make shots MOD=mods/square_photo_mode writes them to _metadata/1-<hash>.png … 9-…. Pictures and window captures: plans/square_photo_mode/shots/. Click a slide to see it large.

Poster and cover

1920×1080 on the house cover (teal field, title, tagline) with postcards in place of the pictogram, the same size and ratio as every cover: it is Photo Mode's _metadata/0.png (mod.io logo, shown at 320×180) and works for sharing. make covers MOD=mods/square_photo_mode renders it there; ?poster shows it alone.
At mod.io thumbnail size (320×180).

Name ideas
