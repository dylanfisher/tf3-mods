>
> New here? Start with a brief [introduction](../modding/introduction.md) into modding Transport Fever 3.

Transport Fever 3 features full modding support, empowering the community to create, customize, and expand the experience in countless ways. Mods can be easily [shared](../modding/general/publishing.md) and discovered through [mod.io](https://mod.io/g/transportfever3), making it simple to connect creators and players across platforms.

We're proud to run a Curated Mods Program, highlighting high-quality creations and supporting standout modders within the community. To help you bring your ideas to life, we provide dedicated modding tools and extensive documentation covering everything from getting started to advanced topics.

Mods are a vital part of Transport Fever 3's ecosystem. They inspire innovation, extend longevity, and strengthen the community. We're incredibly grateful to every creator who contributes their time, talent, and passion.
